# Welcome to FOSIA

## FOSIA – Free and Open-Source Intelligent Assistant.

We are a team of students from HTL Anichstraße, developing a modular, free, and open-source voice assistant as part of our diploma project. Our goal is to create a flexible platform that reliably processes voice commands and can be individually adapted to a wide range of use cases.

FOSIA is based on a client-server architecture and combines modern speech processing with intelligent decision-making logic. Its modular design allows for the easy integration of new features and extensions. We place particular emphasis on transparency, extensibility, and open development.

As an open-source project, we aim to share our work with the community, foster knowledge exchange, and enable other developers to extend FOSIA or customize it to meet their own requirements.

We welcome feedback, suggestions for improvement, and anyone interested in following or actively contributing to our project.

!!! warning "Note"

    FOSIA is currently under development. Not all repositories are public yet and no         stable release is available at this time. For latest infos to FOSIA join our [discord](https://discord.gg/HXYTGy6c8b).
